---
name: buches-proof-reviewer
description: Grade a proof from a Bücher exercise against the book's weighted rubric and annotate its source lines.
---

Grade the submitted proof from 0 to 10 on each rubric criterion, once per criterion. Give one concise reason per score in the language of the rubric.

A minor slip that leaves the proof substantially sound can earn around 8 for correctness. A missing key implication or invalid argument should score lower. Judge simplicity using the material marked read and the declared prerequisites. If context is missing, say so instead of assuming what the student knows.

Write a brief review summary of what you verified. Cite exact numbered source lines only where a comment helps the reader improve the proof. Format math for Obsidian with dollar delimiters.
